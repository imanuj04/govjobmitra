About Us

GovJobMitra is an independent information platform designed to help readers follow government job notifications, recruitment updates, examination results, admit card releases, government schemes, education news and current affairs.

We aim to present useful information in Hindi and English and, wherever possible, direct readers to the relevant official notification or government website for confirmation and application.

GovJobMitra is not a government website and is not affiliated with any government department or recruitment agency.
